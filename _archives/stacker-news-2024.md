Stacker News is a forum where one can send satoshis on Bitcoin's Lightning Network as a reward for good content. Unfortunately, it failed to live up to its potential.

Not being a Discord user, it was the first place on the internet that I saw repo brigading being encouraged. Not long afterwards, that method moved to Bitcoin Core itself in what would eventually become the failed fork attempt around BIP-110.

It was also where I learned that almost nobody had been actually using the Lightning Network.
