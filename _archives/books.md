An inventory of books boxed for shipping, taken from a packing list to be provided to the Customs department in El Salvador.

Eventually, I didn't move ahead with a visa application there on the advice of the legal firm that handled gringo applications. The tax they wanted to impose was also absurdly high using their own estimated values, and the shipping was complicated due to the restrictions in place at that time.

I had previously failed to ship the same package correctly to Romania, which I had moved to in the hope they would react most appropriately to the imposition of such blatantly communist governance. The post-Brexit situation was very unsettled and they released the shipment outside of process but kept me under house arrest as an unvaccinated reactionary.

My next attempt some years later in another EU member nation was less successful. The system doesn't provide for movement of goods outside the corporate import/export framework, so you need to register as an importer. This was technically not possible on the government website in the absence of national ID number held by citizens.

A third attempt in a third EU member nation was unsuccessful for the same reason, although this time there was no pretense that I would be able to make a declaration on my behalf. I was informed I was required to appoint a local Customs agent to provide the documentation within a short window, on expiry of which the shipment would be destroyed.

Not wishing to reward extortion and corruption, I opted for the shipment to be destroyed.
